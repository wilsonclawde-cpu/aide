About Aide

Aide is how TAG Sleaford shares what works. We sell to customers across Europe ourselves, so we know what a quiet week costs and what actually brings customers in.

We check your website, your online listings and the businesses down the road, then send a plain-English report with the fixes that matter. Fixed prices are shown before you buy, every finding comes from something we checked (with the date), and a real person replies when you write.

The Lincolnshire Directory

A free directory of local businesses in Sleaford, Grantham, Boston and Lincoln. Tell us what you need and we show you who is nearby. Listing details come from Food Standards Agency data (Open Government Licence v3.0) and OpenStreetMap contributors, and unclaimed listings may be out of date. You can claim, correct or remove a listing at any time by emailing aide@tagsleaford.com.
